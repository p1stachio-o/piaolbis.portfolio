The Strategy Behind Abante

01

About the Project

Abante is a concept motorcycle ride-booking business created to demonstrate how strategic social media content can help a local transportation service build its online presence and connect with potential customers.

02

Project Goal

To increase brand awareness, introduce the ride-booking service, and encourage potential customers to inquire about bookings.

03

Target Audience

People looking for convenient transportation for their daily commutes, errands, and other local trips.

04

Content Strategy

Create clear, engaging promotional content that highlights the service, communicates its value, and explains how customers can book a ride.

05

Caption & SEO Strategy

Use relevant transportation keywords, location-specific phrases where appropriate, and clear calls to action to help make the content more discoverable and encourage inquiries.

06

Call to Action

Invite interested customers to send a direct message to learn more about the service or inquire about booking a ride.

07

My Role

Social media content planning, graphic design, caption writing, and content strategy.
